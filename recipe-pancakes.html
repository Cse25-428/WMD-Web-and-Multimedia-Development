Fluffy Pancakes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp melted butter

Instructions

  1. Mix dry ingredients in a bowl.
  2. Whisk milk, egg, and melted butter separately.
  3. Combine wet and dry – don't overmix.
  4. Cook on a hot griddle until bubbles form, then flip.
  5. Serve with syrup or fresh fruit.
