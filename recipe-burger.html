Classic Beef Burger

Ingredients

  • 500g ground beef (80/20)
  • Salt and pepper
  • 4 burger buns
  • Lettuce leaves
  • Tomato slices
  • Cheese slices (optional)
  • Ketchup & mustard

Instructions

  1. Form beef into 4 patties, season generously.
  2. Cook on hot grill or pan – 3-4 min per side.
  3. Toast buns lightly.
  4. Assemble: bottom bun, lettuce, patty, cheese, tomato, sauce, top bun.
  5. Serve immediately.
