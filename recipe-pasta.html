Creamy Garlic Pasta

Ingredients

  • 200g pasta (fettuccine or spaghetti)
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan
  • Salt, pepper, parsley

Instructions

  1. Cook pasta according to package.
  2. In a pan, melt butter and sauté garlic until fragrant.
  3. Add cream and simmer 2-3 minutes.
  4. Stir in Parmesan until melted.
  5. Toss with cooked pasta, season, garnish with parsley.
